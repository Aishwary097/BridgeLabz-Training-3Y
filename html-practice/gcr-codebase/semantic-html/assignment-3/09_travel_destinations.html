Top 3 Travel Destinations

Three memorable places to explore in India.

Agra, Uttar Pradesh

Agra is home to the Taj Mahal, a white-marble monument known for its symmetry, gardens, and detailed craftsmanship.

Visiting early in the morning offers a quieter view of the monument.

Travel note

Jaipur, Rajasthan

Jaipur pairs historic forts and palaces with lively markets, traditional crafts, and colourful Rajasthani cuisine.

Leave time to explore the old city on foot and discover its small workshops.

Travel note

Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Varanasi is an ancient city on the Ganges, with riverside ghats, narrow lanes, and a rich tradition of music and learning.

A respectful walk along the ghats reveals the city's everyday rhythm.

Travel note

Travel Tips

  • Check local weather and travel advisories.
  • Dress comfortably for walking.
  • Respect local customs and photography guidance.
